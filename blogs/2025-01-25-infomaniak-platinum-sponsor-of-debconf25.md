We are pleased to announce that Infomaniak has committed to sponsor DebConf25 as a Platinum Sponsor . Infomaniak is Switzerland’s leading developer of Web technologies. With operations all over Europe and based exclusively in Switzerland, the company designs and manages its own data centers powered by 100% renewable energy, and develops all its solutions locally, without outsourcing.
