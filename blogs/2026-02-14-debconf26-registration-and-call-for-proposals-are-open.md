Registration and the Call for Proposals for DebConf26 are now open. The 27th edition of the Debian annual conference will be held from July 20th to July 25th, 2026, in Santa Fe, Argentina. The conference days will be preceded by DebCamp, which will take place from July 13th to July 19th, 2026.
