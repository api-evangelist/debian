We are pleased to announce that Proxmox has committed to sponsor DebConf25 as a Platinum Sponsor . Proxmox develops powerful, yet easy-to-use Open Source server software. The product portfolio from Proxmox, including server virtualization, backup, and email security, helps companies of any size, sector, or industry to simplify their IT infrastructures.
